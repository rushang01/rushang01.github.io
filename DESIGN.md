# Design system

**Concept: the engineering pad.** Data engineers think on paper and whiteboards before they build. The site is a stack of green engineering-pad sheets with real systems sketched on them. Each section makes one point, then stops.

Inspired by two references Rushang shared: the tactile graph paper and collage of johnnyharris.ch, and the confident single-idea sections of apple.com. Nothing is copied from either.

The direction was set from those two references and Rushang's brief, not a survey of other portfolio sites.

## Rules

- Spend boldness in one place: sketched diagrams of real systems. Everything else stays quiet.
- Every diagram shows something real. No decorative charts, and no numbers without a source.
- One idea per section, with a plain heading that says what it is.
- Handwriting is for annotation only, the way an engineer marks up a drawing. It never carries body text or navigation.
- One motion moment: the home schematic draws itself as it scrolls in. Nothing else animates on its own.

## Type

- **Hanken Grotesk** (variable): everything. Headlines are tight and heavy, in the Apple manner.
- **Architects Daughter**: annotation inside diagrams and margin notes only.

Scale: 14, 16, 18, 21, 28, 40, 64, and fluid display up to 112px. Body text is 18px, with lines under 70 characters.

## Spacing and shape

- 8px base. The graph runs on a 24px grid, with heavier lines every 120px.
- Sections use 96 to 160px vertical padding. Content width is 1200px.
- Sheets have 2px corners, one shadow and at most 1.5° of rotation. Pills are used only for the primary button.

## Components

- **Primary button:** ink pill with pad-colored text.
- **Quiet link:** ink text with a red pencil underline.
- **Sheet:** paper with a tape strip, used for covers and notes.
- **Sketch boxes:** generated by `src/lib/sketch.ts` with a seeded wobble, so builds are repeatable.
- **Note:** a handwritten margin note in red pencil.

## Avoid

Gradients, glass, emoji icons, identical three-card rows, all-caps eyebrows, numbered section markers, stock photos, fade-up on scroll, and arrows appended to links.
